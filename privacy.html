finlab-backup 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-07

  • 本應用程式只供擁有者本人使用：由擁有者自己電腦上的 rclone，把擁有者自己的檔案加密後備份到擁有者自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。
  • 不收集、不保存、不分析任何其他人的資料。
  • 不把任何資料傳送、出售或分享給第三方。
  • 透過 Google 授權取得的存取權只用於上述備份與還原，授權權杖只存在擁有者自己的電腦上。
  • 擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」頁面撤銷授權。

Privacy policy (English)

  • This app is used only by its owner: rclone on the owner's own computer encrypts the owner's own files and backs them up to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, store or analyse anyone else's data.
  • No data is sent, sold or shared with any third party.
  • Access granted through Google sign-in is used only for this backup and restore; the token stays on the owner's computer.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account third-party access page.
